# TauGrid v0.4.9

## Guaranteed team quotas and shared spare capacity

- Opt into `TauCluster.spec.queues.shareTeamQuotas` to retain explicit
  `TauTeam.spec.quota` guarantees while sharing unused quota between teams.
  Workspaces share their team's entitlement without manual quota splits.
- The parent Cohort contains only discovered capacity left after team
  guarantees. Ordered transfers avoid counting parent and team budgets twice.
  Ancillary resources without team guarantees remain shared at the parent.
- Borrowed jobs are not evicted to reclaim another team's guarantee. Entitled
  jobs wait for borrowed jobs to finish. Nominal quota is not a hard usage cap.
- Inventory or ownership failures and physical shortages hold new admissions
  without evicting admitted jobs. Kueue Hold cancels quota-only reservations.
- Exact hardware-label selectors classify GPU nodes without requiring an
  instance-type label. Unclassified eligible GPU nodes block whole-pool
  accounting rather than silently disappearing.
- Connected, verified team workspaces can submit explicit-resource Jobs
  without presets when the workload-profile catalog is empty.

## Compatibility and upgrade

The opt-in is disabled by default and mutually exclusive with the released
exclusive `capacityTeamRef` mode. Existing explicit quota behavior is unchanged.
Upgrade the controller, CRDs, and coordinated charts together. Drain and zero
an exclusive budget before opting into shared team quotas. Same-workspace-UID
team reassignment preserves the existing workload namespace and its storage.

This is a new release candidate; v0.4.8 artifacts remain immutable. Publish only
after reviewed merge, coordinated image/chart verification, and the normal
release gates. Source preparation does not authorize or perform deployment.
