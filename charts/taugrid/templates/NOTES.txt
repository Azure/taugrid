Helm owns cluster-scoped installation resources. TauWorkspace reconciliation
owns workspace namespaces, access, LocalQueues, and optional workload identity
ServiceAccounts. StorageClasses, PVCs, PVs, CSI configuration, cloud storage,
and data lifecycle remain platform-owned and are not removed by TauGrid.
